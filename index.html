Mind Maps

Turn PowerPoint decks into editable mind maps. Your edits save automatically on this device.

Saved on this device
To install: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. The app then opens full screen and works offline. Maps are stored on this device, so use Export on a card to keep a backup.

With AI on, the text and speaker notes of each deck are sent to Anthropic to build the map. Your key stays on this device and goes only to api.anthropic.com. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account. Without a key, imports use a basic offline draft.

Tap to select · double-tap to rename · pinch to zoom · drag to pan · Tab / Enter with a keyboard
